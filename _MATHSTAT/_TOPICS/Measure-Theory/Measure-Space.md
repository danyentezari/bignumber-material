# Measure Space

A *space* is a set with certain conditions. In mathematics, a space is denoted as a pair of any elements; for example, $(A,B,...)$  We define a measure space together with its conditions formally here.

<i>

**definition [d]** (*Measure Space*)  
A *measure space* is a space $(E, \mathcal{E}, \mathcal{M} )$ where:

- $E$ is a set the subsets of which will be measured
- $\mathcal{E}$ is a $\sigma$-algebra of measurable subsets of $E$
- $\mathcal{M}$ is the measure on $(E, \mathcal{E})$

</i>

In this definition, when we say $\mathcal{M}$ is the measure on $(E, \mathcal{E})$, we mean to say $$ \mathcal{M}: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow [0,\infty] $$.

It is important to indicate that the measure is "on" the pair $(E, \mathcal{E})$ so that we know $\mathcal{E}$ are subsets of $E$.
