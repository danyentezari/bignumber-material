# Probability Space

A *probability space* is a special case of a measure space which we define here formally.

<i>

**definition [d]** (*Probability Space*) 

A *probability space* is a measure space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P} )$ where:

- $\Omega$ is the sample space
- $\mathcal{F}$ is the $\sigma$-algebra
- $\mathbb{P}$ is the measure with domain $[0,1]$

</i>
