# Guide

<!-- pdf-page-width: default -->

| Term                                              | Meaning                                                     | Derived in subsection                               |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| \(\mu\)                                           | Mean, centre of the distribution                            | Binomial Distribution → General Normal Distribution |
| \(\sigma\)                                        | Standard deviation, scale                                   | Binomial Distribution → General Normal Distribution |
| \(\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\)                         | Standardized displacement from the mean                     | Standardization                                     |
| \(\left(\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2\)          | Squared standardized displacement                           | De Moivre–Laplace Approximation                     |
| \(-\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2\) | Gaussian exponent                                           | De Moivre–Laplace Approximation                     |
| \(\exp[\cdot]\)                                   | Exponential form arising from the logarithmic approximation | De Moivre–Laplace Approximation                     |
| \(\sqrt{2\pi}\)                                   | Gaussian normalization constant                             | Gaussian Integral                                   |
| \(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\)                        | Standard-normal normalization factor                        | Standard Normal Distribution                        |
| \(\dfrac{1}{\sigma}\)                             | Scaling factor caused by \(x=\mu+\sigma z\)                 | General Normal Distribution                         |
| \(\dfrac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\)                  | Final normalization factor                                  | General Normal Distribution + Gaussian Integral     |
