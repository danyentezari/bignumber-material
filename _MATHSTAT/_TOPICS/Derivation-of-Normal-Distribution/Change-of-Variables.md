# Change of Variables

$$
\begin{align*}
u
&=
g(x)
\tag*{\just{For a one-dimensional integral, let}}
\\[0.45em]
\frac{du}{dx}
&=
g'(x)
\tag*{\just{Then}}
\\[0.45em]
du
&=
g'(x)\,dx
\tag*{\just{and therefore}}
\\[0.45em]
\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx
&=
\int f(u)\,du
\tag*{\just{Thus,}}
\\[0.45em]
\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx
&=
\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du
\tag*{\just{For a definite integral,}}
\end{align*}
$$

The same principle extends to several variables, except that changes in area or volume must also be accounted for.
