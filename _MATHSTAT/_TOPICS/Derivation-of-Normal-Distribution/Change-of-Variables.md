# Change of Variables

For a one-dimensional integral, let

$$
u=g(x).
$$

Then

$$
\frac{du}{dx}
=
g'(x)
$$

and therefore

$$
du
=
g'(x)\,dx.
$$

Thus,

$$
\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx
=
\int f(u)\,du.
$$

For a definite integral,

$$
\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx
=
\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.
$$

The same principle extends to several variables, except that changes in area or volume must also be accounted for.
