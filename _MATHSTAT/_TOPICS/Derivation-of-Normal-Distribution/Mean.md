# Mean

For the standard normal distribution,

$$
E[Z]
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
z e^{-z^2/2}\,dz.
$$

The integrand is an odd function over a symmetric interval. Therefore,

$$
E[Z]
=
0.
$$

Since

$$
X
=
\mu+\sigma Z,
$$

we obtain

$$
E[X]
=
\mu+\sigma E[Z].
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
\mu
}.
$$
