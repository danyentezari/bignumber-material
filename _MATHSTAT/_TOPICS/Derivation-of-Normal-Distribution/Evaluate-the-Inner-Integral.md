# Evaluate the Inner Integral

Let

$$
u
=
\frac{r^2}{2}.
$$

Then

$$
du
=
r\,dr.
$$

Thus,

$$
\int_0^\infty
e^{-r^2/2}
r\,dr
=
\int_0^\infty e^{-u}\,du.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-u}\,du
=
\left[
-e^{-u}
\right]_0^\infty
=
1.
$$

Hence,

$$
I^2
=
\int_0^{2\pi}1\,d\theta.
$$

Therefore,

$$
I^2
=
2\pi.
$$

Since $I>0$,

$$
I
=
\sqrt{2\pi}.
$$

Thus,

$$
\boxed{
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
e^{-x^2/2}\,dx
=
\sqrt{2\pi}
}
$$

and equivalently,

$$
\boxed{
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
e^{-x^2}\,dx
=
\sqrt{\pi}
}.
$$
