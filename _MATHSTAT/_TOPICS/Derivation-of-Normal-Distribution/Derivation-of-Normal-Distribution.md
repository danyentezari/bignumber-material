# Derivation and Proof of the Normal Distribution

<!-- pdf-page-width: wide -->

\defaultwidthpar{The result to be established is}

$$
\begin{align*}
f(x;\mu,\sigma)
&=
\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}
\exp\left[
-\frac{1}{2}
\left(
\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
\right)^2
\right]
\tag*{\just{Normal probability density}}
\\
-\infty
&<
x
<
\infty
\\
\sigma
&>
0
\tag*{\just{Domain and scale conditions}}
\end{align*}
$$

\defaultwidthpar{This derivation establishes the normal distribution from the normal approximation to the binomial distribution and proves that the resulting probability density integrates to one.

Axiomatic Basis. No new axiom is introduced in this derivation. The axioms of probability are assumed as prerequisites.}
