# Definition 2: Standard Normal Distribution

<!-- pdf-page-width: wide -->

Definition. A random variable $Z$ has the standard normal distribution if

$$
\begin{align*}
Z
&\sim
N(0,1)
\tag*{\just{Standard normal random variable}}
\\[0.45em]
f_Z(z)
&=
\phi(z)
\\
&=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
e^{-z^2/2}
\tag*{\just{Its probability density}}
\end{align*}
$$

for

$$
\begin{align*}
-\infty
&<
z
<
\infty.
\tag*{\just{Support of the standard normal distribution}}
\end{align*}
$$
