# Definition 3: General Normal Distribution

<!-- pdf-page-width: wide -->

Definition. Let

$$
\begin{align*}
Z
&\sim
N(0,1)
\tag*{\just{Start from a standard normal random variable}}
\\[0.45em]
X
&=
\mu+\sigma Z,
\qquad
\sigma>0
\tag*{\just{Translate and scale the standard normal variable}}
\end{align*}
$$

Then $X$ has a normal distribution with parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$, written

$$
\begin{align*}
X
&\sim
N(\mu,\sigma^2).
\tag*{\just{General normal distribution}}
\end{align*}
$$
