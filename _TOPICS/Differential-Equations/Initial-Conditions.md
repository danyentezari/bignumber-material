# Initial Conditions

A value of a function at a fixed point of its domain that is used to determine a unique solution of an equation.

<i>

**definition [d]** (*Initial Condition*) From Stewart: in many physical problems we need to find the particular solution that satisfies a condition of the form

- $y(t_{0}) = y_{0}$ .

This is called an initial condition, and the problem of finding a solution of the differential equation that satisfies the initial condition is called an initial-value problem.

where

- $y$ is the unknown function.
- $t_{0}$ is a fixed value of the independent variable.
- $y_{0}$ is the prescribed value of $y$ at $t_{0}$.

</i>
