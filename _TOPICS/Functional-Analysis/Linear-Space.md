# Linear Space

<i>

**definition [d]** (*Linear Space = Vector Space*) A nonempty set $X$ of elements called vectors, together with vector addition and multiplication by scalars from a field $K$, satisfying the vector-space axioms.

where

- $X$ is the linear space.
- $K$ is the scalar field.
- the two algebraic operations are vector addition and scalar multiplication.

Note:

- Kreyszig treats “vector space” and “linear space” as synonyms.
- $K$ is typically $\mathbb{R}$.
- $K$ is also typically $\mathbb{C}$.

</i>

<i>

**definition [d]** (*Linear Space = Vector Space = Linear Vector Space*) A set $V$ of objects called vectors, denoted $|a\rangle$, $|b\rangle$, $|x\rangle$, and so on, over $\mathbb{C}$, with the algebraic properties of a vector space.

where

- $V$ is the vector space.
- $|a\rangle, |b\rangle, |x\rangle$ are vectors in $V$.
- $\mathbb{C}$ is the field of complex scalars.

Note:

- Hassani notes the common practice of saying “vector space” in place of the more formal “linear vector space.”
- Hassani also notes the practice of saying “space” in that same sense.

</i>

## Examples

<i>

**example [d]** (**Infinite-Dimensional Coordinate Space** — Hassani) As a set,

- $\mathbb{C}^{\infty} = \left\{ |a\rangle = \{\alpha_{i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty} \;\middle|\; \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |\alpha_{i}|^{2} < \infty \right\}$ ,

with componentwise vector addition and scalar multiplication. This is an infinite-dimensional complex linear space.

where

- $\mathbb{C}^{\infty}$ is the infinite-dimensional complex coordinate space.
- $|a\rangle = \{\alpha_{i}\}$ is a square-summable sequence of complex numbers.
- $\alpha_{i} \in \mathbb{C}$ are the components.

Note:

- the same set, equipped with the $\ell^{2}$ inner product, is a Hilbert space.

</i>
