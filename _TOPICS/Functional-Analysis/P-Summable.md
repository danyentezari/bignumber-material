# P-Summable

A property of a sequence under which the series of absolute values raised to a parameter power converges. Used to decide which sequences belong to the matching set of sequences.

<i>

**definition [d]** (*p-Summable*) A property of a sequence $x = (\xi_{j})$ of scalars: the series of $p$-th powers converges,

- $\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty} |\xi_{j}|^{p} < \infty$ ,

for a fixed real number $p \geq 1$.

where

- $x = (\xi_{j})$ is a sequence of scalars.
- $p \geq 1$ is a fixed real number.
- $|\xi_{j}|$ is the absolute value of the $j$-th term.

Note:

- a sequence with this property is an element of $\ell^{p}$.

</i>

<i>

**definition [d]** (*p-Summable*) A property of a sequence $x = \{x_{j}\}_{j=1}^{\infty}$: its $p$-norm is finite,

- $\lVert x \rVert_{p} = \left( \displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty} |x_{j}|^{p} \right)^{\dfrac{1}{p}} < \infty$ ,

for $1 \leq p < \infty$.

where

- $x = \{x_{j}\}$ is a sequence of scalars.
- $\lVert x \rVert_{p}$ is the $p$-norm of $x$.
- $p$ is a real number with $1 \leq p < \infty$.

Note:

- the scalars may be real.
- the scalars may be complex.
- finiteness of $\lVert x \rVert_{p}$ is equivalent to $\sum |x_{j}|^{p} < \infty$.

</i>
