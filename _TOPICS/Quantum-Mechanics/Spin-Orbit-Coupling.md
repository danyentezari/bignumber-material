# Spin-Orbit Coupling

The interaction between a particle's orbital angular momentum and its spin angular momentum, coupling its spatial motion with its intrinsic spin.

**definition [d]** (*Spin-Orbit Coupling*) — Interaction between a particle's intrinsic spin and its orbital angular momentum, often expressed as an L·S term.
*Ref:* Arfken, Weber & Harris, *Mathematical Methods for Physicists*, 7th ed. (2013) [citing Condon & Shortley, *Theory of Atomic Spectra*]; "illinois-covariant-contravariant.pdf".
