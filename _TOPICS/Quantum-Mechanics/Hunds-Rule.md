# Hund's Rule

A guideline that is used to occupy degenerate orbitals singly with parallel spins before pairing, where degenerate orbitals are orbitals that share the same energy.

1\. Electrons in a subshell occupy different orbitals with parallel spins until each orbital has one electron. This principle is used to maximize the total spin $S$ of the ground term.

2\. For a given $S$, the term with the largest orbital angular momentum $L$ lies lowest. This principle is used to choose the ground term among terms of the same spin.

3\. For a subshell that is more than half full, the term with larger $J$ lies lower; for a less than half-full subshell, the smaller $J$ lies lower. This principle is used to assign the ground $J$ value.

Note: These principles are maximum multiplicity, maximum $L$, and the $J$ ordering of Hund's third rule.
