# Aufbau Principle

A systematic rule that is used to build the ground-state electron configuration of an atom by filling subshells in order of increasing energy, where a subshell is the set of orbitals sharing $n$ and $\ell$.

1\. Electrons occupy the lowest available subshells first. This principle is used to write the ground configuration from hydrogen through the heavier atoms.

2\. The usual filling order follows increasing $n+\ell$, and for equal $n+\ell$ the smaller $n$ fills first. This principle is used to remember the sequence $1s$, $2s$, $2p$, $3s$, $3p$, $4s$, $3d$.

3\. Each orbital holds at most two electrons of opposite spin. This principle is used together with the Pauli principle to stop filling a subshell when it is full.

Note: These principles are lowest-energy filling, the $n+\ell$ order, and the two-electron orbital capacity. Aufbau is German for building up.
