# Spin

The intrinsic angular momentum carried by elementary particles, an inherent quantum property with no classical counterpart from physical rotation.

**definition [d]** (*Electron Spin = Spin Angular Momentum = Intrinsic Angular Momentum*) — Intrinsic angular momentum of an electron, characterized by quantum number 1/2 and represented mathematically by spinors.
*Ref:* Arfken, Weber & Harris, *Mathematical Methods for Physicists*, 7th ed. (2013).
