# Work

A measure of energy transferred by a force acting through a displacement that is used to track energy handed from one system to another.

Note: Everyday "work" is broader. In physics, work means energy transfer of this specific kind.

1. Is the mechanical process of transferring energy to or from a system.
2. Equals the line integral of force along a trajectory.
3. Uses only the force component along the displacement.
4. Is a scalar, even though it is built from vector force and displacement.
5. Is zero when the force is perpendicular to the displacement.
6. Cannot be done on a moving charge by a magnetic force, which stays perpendicular to velocity.
7. Equals the change in kinetic energy under a net force.
8. Depends on path for nonconservative forces, but only on endpoints for conservative forces.
9. Sums to zero around any closed loop for conservative forces.
10. For gas compression equals minus pressure times the change in volume.
