# Mass

A measure of an object's resistance to changes in its motion that is used to tell how stubbornly it keeps its current velocity.

Note: In everyday language mass is often mixed up with weight. Weight is the gravitational pull on an object. Mass is a property of the object itself.

1. Quantifies resistance to acceleration under a net force.
2. Sets the strength of mutual gravitational attraction between bodies.
3. Is relativistically invariant under Lorentz transformations between inertial frames.
4. Equals rest energy divided by the square of the speed of light.
5. Is an intrinsic scalar property of matter.
6. Matches gravitational mass with inertial mass by the equivalence principle.
7. Is not conserved in relativity: rest mass and kinetic energy can convert.
8. Determines a system's center of mass from its mass distribution.
9. Acts as a primary source of spacetime curvature in general relativity.
10. Is exactly zero for photons, which always move at the speed of light.
