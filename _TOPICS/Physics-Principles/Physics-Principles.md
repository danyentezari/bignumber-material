# Physics Principles

The principles of physics are the shared rules and vocabulary used to describe the systems and interactions of the natural world.

An interaction is an influence: a mutual push or pull between objects. A system is an entity: a chosen collection of objects studied as one unit.

The principles of physics are related to conservation of energy and momentum because those totals for an isolated system are independent of the path taken and of the internal details of the process.
