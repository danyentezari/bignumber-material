A field describes how the influence of an object is distributed through space.
A potential is the measure of the influence at a point in space.
A potential difference is the measure of the difference in influence between two points in space.
A force is the interaction that changes the motion of an object.
Energy is the measure of a system's capacity to produce physical change.
Work is the measure of energy transferred between systems.
Power is the measure of the rate at which energy is transferred or transformed.
Momentum is the measure of the motion of an object.
Mass is the measure of an object's resistance to changes in its motion.
Charge is the measure of an object's participation in electromagnetic interactions.
