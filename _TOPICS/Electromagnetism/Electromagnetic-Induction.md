# Electromagnetic Induction

The process by which a changing magnetic field, or a conductor moving through a magnetic field, generates an electromotive force and can drive a current.

Note: Also called induction. See [Induction](Induction.md).

($\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\dfrac{\partial\mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$) (Faraday's law).
