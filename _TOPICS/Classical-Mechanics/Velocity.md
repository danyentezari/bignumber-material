# Velocity

A vector rate of change of position that is used to describe how fast and in which direction an object moves.

Note: Also called the velocity vector. Also called instantaneous velocity.

1. Is the rate of change of the position vector with respect to time.
2. Is defined by $\mathbf{v} = \dfrac{d\mathbf{r}}{dt}$.
3. Has components $\dfrac{dx}{dt}$, $\dfrac{dy}{dt}$, and $\dfrac{dz}{dt}$ in Cartesian coordinates.
4. Points along the path of motion in the limit of a vanishing time interval.
5. Is a vector because it is the limit of a change of position vectors.
6. Serves as the starting variable for acceleration.

3. Shankar, R. *Fundamentals of Physics I*. Yale University Press, 2019. — $\mathbf{v}=\lim_{\Delta t\rightarrow 0}\dfrac{\Delta\mathbf{r}}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{d\mathbf{r}}{dt}$.
