# Classical Mechanics

The theory of motion of bodies under forces at ordinary speeds, based on Newton’s laws and later Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formulations. Mathematically, classical mechanics fundamentally deals with differential equations, whereas quantum mechanics fundamentally deals with probability theory.

We have this section for classical mechanics as a foundation for quantum mechanics, because the former establishes the concepts of energy and motion, and for its relevance to differential equations.
