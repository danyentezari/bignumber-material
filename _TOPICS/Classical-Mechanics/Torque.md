# Torque

A vector measure of a force’s turning effect that is used to describe how a force tends to change angular momentum about an origin.

Note: Also denoted $\boldsymbol{\tau}$. Also called the moment of a force.

1. Is defined by $\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F}$.
2. Depends on the choice of origin through the position vector $\mathbf{r}$.
3. Has magnitude $r F \sin\gamma$, where $\gamma$ is the angle from $\mathbf{r}$ to $\mathbf{F}$.
4. Points perpendicular to the plane of $\mathbf{r}$ and $\mathbf{F}$ by the right-hand rule.
5. Equals the rate of change of angular momentum about the same origin.
6. Vanishes for the total external torque when total angular momentum is conserved.
