# Acceleration

A rate of change of velocity that is used to describe how quickly an object’s motion changes.

Note: Also called the acceleration vector. Also written as the second derivative of position.

1. Is the derivative of velocity with respect to time.
2. Is also the second derivative of position with respect to time.
3. Is defined by $a = \dfrac{dv}{dt} = \dfrac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}}$ in one dimension.
4. Is zero when velocity is constant.
5. Enters Newton’s second law as the motion side of $\mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$.
6. Points in the direction of the change of the velocity vector.
