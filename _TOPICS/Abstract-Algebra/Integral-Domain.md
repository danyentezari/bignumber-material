# Integral Domain

A set with addition and multiplication like the integers, with no nonzero product equal to zero that is used to solve polynomial equations.

<i>

**definition** (*Integral Domain*) A type of ring with axioms (1) to (8) but not (7).

Note:

- axioms (1) to (8) are the ring axioms.
- axiom (7) is unity, the multiplicative identity.

</i>
