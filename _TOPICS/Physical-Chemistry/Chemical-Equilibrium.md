# Chemical Equilibrium

A balanced state of a reacting system that is used to predict the final amounts of reactants and products.

Note: Atkins identifies the balance with the mixture at its lowest Gibbs energy.

1. A reaction consumes reactants and creates products, so reactant amounts fall and product amounts rise.
2. The process reaches a point where the net amounts of all substances stop changing.
3. That standstill leaves no leftover drive to change the composition either way.
4. The reaction has not stopped: reactants still form products and products still form reactants at equal speeds.
5. Equilibrium is used to find the greatest possible yield and to predict how temperature and pressure change the final mixture.
