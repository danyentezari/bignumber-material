# Four-Current

A four-vector formed from charge density and current density that is used to package the sources of the electromagnetic field into one Lorentz-covariant object.

Note: Also called the current $4$-vector. Also called the current density $4$-vector.

The four-current is

$$
J^{\mu} = (c\rho,\, \mathbf{J})
$$

where

- $J^{\mu}$ is the four-current.
- $\rho$ is the charge density.
- $\mathbf{J}$ is the current density.
- $c$ is the speed of light.
