# Electromagnetic Field Transformations

The rules for how electric and magnetic fields transform between inertial frames, so a pure electric field in one frame can appear mixed with a magnetic field in another.

($\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$) (Lorentz force).
