# Rapidity

A hyperbolic parameter for a boost that is used to rewrite Lorentz transformations so successive boosts add by summing parameters.

Note: Also denoted $\psi$. Also denoted $\theta$. Also denoted $\phi$.

The rapidity $\psi$ satisfies

$$
\tanh\psi = \beta
$$

$$
\cosh\psi = \gamma
$$

$$
\sinh\psi = \beta\gamma
$$

where

- $\psi$ is the rapidity.
- $\gamma$ is the Lorentz factor.
- $\beta = \dfrac{v}{c}$ is the relative speed in units of $c$.
- $v$ is the boost speed.
- $c$ is the speed of light.
