# Lorentz Transformations

The equations that relate the space and time coordinates of an event in two inertial frames moving at constant relative velocity that is used to convert spacetime coordinates between inertial frames.

($x' = \gamma(x - vt),\quad t' = \gamma\left(t - \dfrac{vx}{c^2}\right)$).
