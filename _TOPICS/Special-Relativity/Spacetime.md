# Spacetime

A four-dimensional set of events that is used to label occurrences by three dimensions of space and one of time.

Note: Also called space-time. An individual point in spacetime is called an event.

## References

1. Carroll, S. M. *Spacetime and Geometry*. Cambridge University Press, 2021. — spacetime as a four-dimensional set of events.
2. Knight, R. D. *Physics for Scientists and Engineers: A Strategic Approach with Modern Physics*. Pearson, 2023. — spacetime coordinates of an event.
