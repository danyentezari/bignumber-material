# Total Energy

A relativistic energy equal to the Lorentz factor times rest energy that is used to express the full energy of a free particle including rest energy and kinetic energy.

Note: Also called relativistic energy.

The total energy is

$$
E = \gamma mc^{2} = E_{0} + K
$$

where

- $E$ is the total energy.
- $\gamma$ is the Lorentz factor of the particle.
- $m$ is the rest mass.
- $c$ is the speed of light.
- $E_{0} = mc^{2}$ is the rest energy.
- $K$ is the kinetic energy.
