# Events

A physical occurrence at a definite place and time that is used to label points of spacetime by four coordinates.

Note: Also called a spacetime event. Also called a spacetime point.

The spacetime coordinates of an event are

$$
(x, y, z, t)
$$

where

- $(x, y, z)$ are the spatial coordinates of the event.
- $t$ is the time of the event.
